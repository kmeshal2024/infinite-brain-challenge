# Privacy Policy for Infinite Brain Challenge

**Effective Date:** April 16, 2026
**Last Updated:** April 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Infinite Brain Challenge** ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:
- Your real name, email address, phone number, or postal address
- Photos, contacts, microphone audio, or camera input
- GPS or precise location data
- Browsing history outside the App
- Any information about other apps on your device

### 1.2 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following **on your device only**:
- Game progress (levels completed, stars earned, scores)
- In-game coins and virtual currency
- App preferences (sound, vibration, notifications)
- Daily login streak and last played date
- Achievement unlock status

This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

### 1.3 Information Collected by Third-Party SDKs

The App integrates the following third-party services:

**AppLovin MAX (Advertising)**
- Purpose: Shows ads inside the App to support free development
- Data collected: device advertising ID, device type, OS version, approximate location (country-level), IP address, in-app interaction events
- Users may opt out of personalized advertising in their device settings
- Privacy Policy: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/

**Google Play Services**
- Purpose: In-app review requests and Play Store integration
- Data collected: basic device information and Play Store account status
- Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used to:
- Provide and maintain the App's functionality
- Save your progress between sessions
- Display relevant advertisements
- Improve app performance and user experience
- Comply with legal obligations

We do **not** sell or rent your information to any third party.

---

## 3. Advertising

We display advertisements through AppLovin MAX. These ads are filtered to be **family-friendly** (G-rated):

- No alcohol, tobacco, drug, or gambling ads
- No adult or dating content
- No violent, horror, or mature movie trailers
- No political or religious advertisements

You can opt out of personalized advertising by:
- **Android:** Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
- Resetting your advertising ID at any time

Opting out will still show ads, but they will not be personalized.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences. We apply the following protections:

- All ads are filtered to G-rated content
- No collection of personal information from any user
- The COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) "age-restricted" flag is passed to our ad partners
- We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13

If a parent or guardian believes that their child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below, and we will delete that information promptly.

---

## 5. Data Security

Your game data is stored locally on your device using encrypted preferences. Because no personal data is transmitted to our servers, there is no personal data for us to protect on our end.

Our ad partners (AppLovin) use industry-standard security measures to protect advertising data.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your region (GDPR in Europe, CCPA in California, etc.), you may have the following rights:

- **Access:** Request a copy of the data processed about you
- **Deletion:** Request that your data be deleted
- **Opt-out:** Opt out of personalized advertising
- **Portability:** Request data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

You can also delete all local app data at any time by:
- Uninstalling the App
- Clearing App data: Settings → Apps → Infinite Brain Challenge → Storage → Clear Data
