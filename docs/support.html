Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my progress?

Go to your device Settings > Apps > Infinite Brain Challenge > Clear Data. This will reset all progress.

Does the app work offline?

Yes! All games work fully offline. An internet connection is only needed for loading fonts on first launch.

How do I earn more coins?

Complete levels to earn coins. Perfect scores give bonus coins. Daily challenges offer extra rewards. Maintain your streak for bonus multipliers.

Is the app suitable for children?

Yes! Infinite Brain Challenge is rated for Everyone. It contains no violence, no in-app purchases, and no ads in the current version.

My progress disappeared. What happened?

Game progress is stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the app or clear app data, progress will be lost. Cloud saves are planned for a future update.

Will there be new games added?

Yes! We're working on additional brain-training games. Stay tuned for updates.
