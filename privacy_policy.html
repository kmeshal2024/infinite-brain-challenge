Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

Infinite Brain Challenge ("we", "our", or "the app") is a brain-training game developed by Infinite Brain. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Summary: Infinite Brain Challenge is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All game data is stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Infinite Brain Challenge does not collect any personal information. The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Game progress and scores
  • In-app currency balances (coins, stars, hints)
  • User preferences and settings
  • Daily challenge completion status

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

2. Data Storage

All data is stored locally using your device's secure storage mechanisms (SharedPreferences on Android, UserDefaults on iOS). This data persists between app sessions but is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear app data.

3. Third-Party Services

The current version (v1.0.0) of Infinite Brain Challenge does not integrate any third-party services such as analytics, advertising networks, or crash reporting tools.

Future versions may integrate services such as:

  • Firebase Analytics (for anonymous usage analytics)
  • Google AdMob (for displaying advertisements)

If and when such services are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly, and you will be notified through an app update.

4. Internet Permission

The app requests the INTERNET permission to:

  • Load custom fonts (Google Fonts)
  • Support future online features

No personal data is transmitted over the internet.

5. Children's Privacy

Infinite Brain Challenge is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since no personal data is collected from any user, the app complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar regulations.

6. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device and no data is transmitted to external servers, the security of your data depends on the security of your device. We recommend:

  • Keeping your device's operating system up to date
  • Using a screen lock on your device
  • Not rooting or jailbreaking your device

7. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access: Your game data is visible within the app's profile and settings screens.
  • Deletion: You can delete all app data by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.
  • Portability: Since data is stored locally, it exists only on your device.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the app's data practices, please contact us at:
